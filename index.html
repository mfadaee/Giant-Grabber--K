How to play

  1. Put in a coin.
  2. Move the claw with ◀ ▶ before the timer runs out, then press DROP.
  3. Grab a toy, then tap it to play for 5 seconds.
  4. Each toy pays more: 5, 10, 15, 20 coins…
  5. Collect every toy in the machine to go up a level. The claw and toys get bigger.
  6. Level 5 has one ginormous Mega Bear. Grab it to win!
  7. Every toy you win rides a pipe to the toy box in your home.
  8. Press Shop to spend coins on upgrades: a stronger grip, a bigger claw, a faster motor, a steadier cable, extra time and the Golden Claw.
  9. Press Home to teleport there. Grab a toy from the box and tap a shelf, the window or the floor to place it. Each toy you place gives you 5 coins!
