# Giant Grabber

A claw-machine game you play in your browser.

**Play it here:** https://mfadaee.github.io/giant-grabber--k/

## How to play

1. Put in a coin.
2. Move the claw with ◀ ▶ before the timer runs out, then press **DROP**.
3. Grab a toy, then tap it to play with it for 5 seconds. Each toy pays more coins: 5, 10, 15, 20…
4. Collect every toy in the machine to go up a level. Each level the claw and the toys get bigger.
5. Level 5 has one ginormous **Mega Bear**. Grab it to win!

## Extras

- **Home:** every toy you win rides a pipe to the toy box in your house. Press **Home** to teleport there, grab a toy from the box and place it on a shelf, the window or the floor for +5 coins.
- **Shop:** spend coins on upgrades: Super Grip, Bigger Claw (grab 2 or 3 toys at once), Turbo Motor, Steady Cable, Extra Time and the Golden Claw.

Keyboard: ← → move · Space drop · C coin

Your progress is saved in the browser you play in.

The whole game is a single file, `index.html`. Open it in any browser to play offline.
